The way down

Each layer is deeper, darker and harder than the last, with its own enemies, ores and bosses.

  1. Depth 0

    Greenreach

    Grass, sky, slimes and zombies. The Slime Guardian blocks the shaft at depth 10.

  2. Depth 12

    Rootdeep

    Old roots and glowing mushrooms. Root creepers, cave spiders and the first lead.

  3. Depth 35

    Glimmer Caverns

    Crystal caves full of stone brutes and crystal slimes. Iron starts here, and Stormgaze is watching.

  4. Depth 55

    Frostvale

    Ice, frost trolls, snow wolves and silver. The Rime Queen rules the bottom.

  5. Depth 90

    Sunscour

    Sand halls under a buried sun. Sand brutes, scorpions, gold, and two bosses made of sand.

  6. Depth 135

    Mirebloom

    A drowned jungle full of bog spitters, mire crawlers and lurkers.

  7. Depth 185

    The Crypt

    Skeletons, wraiths and a colossus made of bone. The Crypt Warden guards the stairs.

  8. Depth 235

    Cinder Depths

    Lava, magma brutes and cinder hounds. At the very bottom waits Cinderheart.
