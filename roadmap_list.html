Roadmap

Where Gloomvein is going · version 0.0.1, early access

OUT NOW

Release 1: The First World

Early access. Everything below is in the game today.

  • Four classes, each its own way to play: Melee, Ranged, Magic and Summoner
  • Eight layers to dig through, from Greenreach down to the Cinder Depths
  • Ten bosses, each a test of your whole build: gear, charms and potions
  • Events: Blood Moon, Ooze Rain, Meteor Shower, the Raider Warband and Crystal Defence, plus Halloween and the Winter Festival
  • Crafting, a furnace you can steer, potions, spells, minions, arrows and mounts
  • A crew that digs for you, even while you are away
  • Rebirth with the Core Tree and Class Mastery, on Normal, Heroic or Mythic
  • 36 achievements, a daily gift and looks for your hero
