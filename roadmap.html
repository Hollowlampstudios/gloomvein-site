Gloomvein roadmap: the road ahead

Read it as a list

Out now: Release 1: The First World

  • Four classes, each its own way to play: Melee, Ranged, Magic and Summoner
  • Eight layers to dig through, from Greenreach down to the Cinder Depths
  • Ten bosses, each a test of your whole build: gear, charms and potions
  • Events: Blood Moon, Ooze Rain, Meteor Shower, the Raider Warband and Crystal Defence, plus Halloween and the Winter Festival
  • Crafting, a furnace you can steer, potions, spells, minions, arrows and mounts
  • A crew that digs for you, even while you are away
  • Rebirth with the Core Tree and Class Mastery, on Normal, Heroic or Mythic
  • 36 achievements, a daily gift and looks for your hero

Coming next: Release 1 updates

  • Helpers who move in: about twelve, with bonuses that work together
  • The Bestiary: record every creature you meet and earn collection bonuses
  • New lands on the surface, and an evil biome that spreads if you let it
  • The Workbench, Anvil and Forge: a full crafting chain
  • The Tinker Bench to combine charms, herbs to gather and an Alchemist
  • More creatures to fight, and mounts you can see under your hero

Release 2: Upheaval

  • A radiant land rises, and spreading biomes fight over the world
  • Eight new bosses
  • New ores from ancient altars, and the gear forged from them
  • About fifty new creatures and new events
  • Wings, armour made for each class, more mounts, potions and charms
  • Fishing
  • More helpers to move in

Release 3: Endgame

  • The final bosses
  • Invaders from the sky and a siege to break
  • Endgame gear
  • Challenge worlds with their own rules
  • Pets
