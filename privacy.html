Gloomvein Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 (player accounts, Google sign-in, cloud save and gameplay statistics added)

Gloomvein is an Android game made by Hollowlamp Studios ("we", "us"). This page explains what the game does with information. The short version: your progress is saved on your phone, with a copy on your player account so it can come back on a new phone. The game gives you an anonymous player account (no name, no email, no password; you may link Google to it) and, unless you turn it off, sends us statistics about how the game is played, such as where players die and where they stop, so we can balance and fix it. You can switch the statistics off and delete your account and its data from the game's Options at any time. The outside companies involved are Supabase, which stores the account and statistics for us, and Google, which shows the optional ads.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (your world, items, coins, gems and settings) is saved in a file on your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes that file. If you use Android's backup, Android may copy it to your Google account; that is Android's feature, not ours.

Cloud save

So that your progress can come back on a new phone, the game also keeps a copy of that save file with your player account (every few minutes while you play, and when you leave the game). It holds only game progress: your hero's name, world, items, coins, gems, upgrades and settings. If you sign in with Google on another phone and a saved game is found, the game asks before loading it. The copy is replaced by each newer one, is not used for statistics, and is deleted with Delete my account and data in Options. Turning statistics off does not stop the cloud save, because it is part of the game, not analytics.

Your player account

The first time you open the game it creates a guest account for you, with no sign-up screen. It is a random id (for example c92e90bb-...), kept on your phone and on our database. A guest account holds no name, email address or password, and we cannot tell who you are from it (if you sign in with Google, see below). We keep with it: when it was made, when the game last connected, your phone's operating system (Android), the game's version, and whether you turned statistics off. We use it to tell one player's statistics apart from another's, and later to let you keep your progress on a new phone if you choose to sign in.

Signing in with Google (optional)

You can choose Sign in with Google when the game first starts or later in Options, so your account can be found again on another phone. Google then shows its own sign-in page and asks you to agree. We receive only your Google account's email address, name and profile picture link, and an id Google uses for our app; we keep the email address and that id with your player account (Supabase stores them for us) and use them only to sign you in. We never post anything to your Google account, never see your Google password, and never use the email for marketing. If the Google account already has a Gloomvein account from another phone, the game signs you in to that one. Delete my account and data in Options removes the email address and the link to Google too; you can also remove Gloomvein's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Gameplay statistics

While Share gameplay statistics is on in Options (it is on unless you turn it off), the game sends small records of what happens in play, linked to your guest account: when you start, pause and come back to the game and how long you were away; when your hero dies (the depth and layer, your class and difficulty, the gear you wore, potions that were active, and which enemy hit you last); when you start, win or lose a boss fight and how long it took; reaching new depths; starting a new world or a rebirth; and watching a rewarded ad. Each record also says which screen was open and the game's version. Records are kept on the phone and sent in batches, so the game works the same without a connection.

We use them only to understand where the game is too hard, too easy or confusing, and to find bugs. We do not use them for advertising, do not sell them, and do not share them with anyone except Supabase, which stores them for us. Records older than 24 months are deleted automatically, and so are guest accounts that have not connected for 24 months.

When the game connects, our database provider sees your phone's internet (IP) address, as every website does. We do not store it with your account or statistics; Supabase keeps it only briefly in its own security logs.

Your choices in the game

  • Share gameplay statistics: OFF. In Options. From then on nothing more is sent, and records still waiting on the phone are thrown away. Your account stays, without new statistics.
  • Delete my account and data. In Options (tap twice to confirm). Your account and every statistics record linked to it are deleted from our database at once, and statistics are turned off. Your progress on the phone is not touched. If you later turn statistics back on, the game makes a new, unrelated guest account.

Where the data is stored

The account, the cloud save, the statistics and the website's launch email list are stored by Supabase on servers in the European Union. Supabase processes them only on our behalf and under its data processing terms. Access is limited by database rules: the game can only add records for its own account and cannot read anyone else's.

If you email us

The game has a Report a problem button that opens your own email app with a message to gloomvein@gmail.com. It fills in the game's version, your phone's model and Android version, your hero's class, difficulty, depth and number of rebirths, so we can look into the problem. You can read and change all of it before you send, and nothing is sent unless you press send. When you write to us, we receive your email address and what you wrote. We use it only to answer you and fix the problem, never for marketing, and we do not share it. We delete the conversation when it is no longer needed to deal with your report.

The launch email list (website)

If you leave your email address in the "Get it on launch day" box on gloomvein.com and tick the box, we keep that address, the time you signed up and which link brought you to the site (for example "reddit"). We use it for one thing: to email you when Gloomvein launches, and then about major releases only if you did not unsubscribe. Every email has an unsubscribe line; reply "unsubscribe" and we delete your address. We never sell or share it. It is stored with our database provider, Supabase (servers in the European Union), which processes it only on our behalf. You can ask us to delete it at any time at gloomvein@gmail.com.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads only, and only when you tap a button to watch one (for example to double your coins or open a free chest). There are no banners and no pop-up ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's own policies describe this: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. If you say no, or have not answered, the game does not request ads.
  • Change your mind. In those places the Rewards screen has an Ad privacy options button that reopens the form at any time.
  • Android settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in Android's settings (Settings, then Privacy, then Ads).
  • No ads at all. Nothing in the game is blocked behind an ad. You can simply never press an ad button.

Purchases

If the game sells gems or removes ads for real money, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your card or account details. Gem packs and what they contain are fixed and shown before you buy. There are no random loot boxes.

Children

Gloomvein is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The guest account and statistics hold no name or contact details. If a child has used the game, Delete my account and data in Options removes everything we hold for that phone, or write to us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under South Africa's POPIA or the EU and UK GDPR) you may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to its use. The quickest way to delete is Delete my account and data in Options. To ask for a copy of your statistics, write to us and include your player id, shown in small print at the bottom of the game's Options screen: without it we cannot tell which records are yours. Our legal basis for statistics is our legitimate interest in balancing and fixing the game, which you can always switch off; for the email list it is your consent. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in South Africa, the Information Regulator). For information that Google holds, use Google's tools at myaccount.google.com and its privacy policy above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. A change that affects what information is collected will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
