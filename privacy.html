Gloomvein Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Gloomvein is an Android game made by Hollowlamp Studios ("we", "us"). This page explains what the game does with information. The short version: the game keeps your progress on your own phone, we run no servers and no accounts, and the only outside company involved is Google, which shows the optional ads. The one thing we store is the website's launch email list, and only if you join it (see below).

What stays on your phone

Your progress (your world, items, coins, gems and settings) is saved in a file on your device. We never receive it. If you use Android's backup, Android may copy that file to your Google account; that is Android's feature, not ours. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools of ours in the game. We do not know who you are or how you play.

If you email us

The game has a Report a problem button that opens your own email app with a message to gloomvein@gmail.com. It fills in the game's version, your phone's model and Android version, your hero's class, difficulty, depth and number of rebirths, so we can look into the problem. You can read and change all of it before you send, and nothing is sent unless you press send. When you write to us, we receive your email address and what you wrote. We use it only to answer you and fix the problem, never for marketing, and we do not share it. We delete the conversation when it is no longer needed to deal with your report.

The launch email list (website)

If you leave your email address in the "Get it on launch day" box on gloomvein.com and tick the box, we keep that address, the time you signed up and which link brought you to the site (for example "reddit"). We use it for one thing: to email you when Gloomvein launches, and then about major releases only if you did not unsubscribe. Every email has an unsubscribe line; reply "unsubscribe" and we delete your address. We never sell or share it. It is stored with our database provider, Supabase (servers in the European Union), which processes it only on our behalf. You can ask us to delete it at any time at gloomvein@gmail.com.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads only, and only when you tap a button to watch one (for example to double your coins or open a free chest). There are no banners and no pop-up ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's own policies describe this: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. If you say no, or have not answered, the game does not request ads.
  • Change your mind. In those places the Rewards screen has an Ad privacy options button that reopens the form at any time.
  • Android settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in Android's settings (Settings, then Privacy, then Ads).
  • No ads at all. Nothing in the game is blocked behind an ad. You can simply never press an ad button.

Purchases

If the game sells gems or removes ads for real money, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your card or account details. Gem packs and what they contain are fixed and shown before you buy. There are no random loot boxes.

Children

Gloomvein is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. As we collect nothing ourselves, there is nothing to delete, but if you believe a child has used the game and you have a concern, write to us.

Your rights

Apart from any email you sent us, we hold no personal information about you. To see or delete our emails, write to the address below. For information that Google holds, use Google's tools at myaccount.google.com and its privacy policy above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. A change that affects what information is collected will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
