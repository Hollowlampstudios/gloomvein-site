Mining

Tap a block to deal your mining damage to it. You start with 5 mining damage and pick power 1.

  • A block can only be broken when your pick power is at least its required power. A column never rolls a new unbreakable block while it already holds 2.
  • Ore luck is the chance that an ore block gives 2 ores instead of 1 (plain blocks never do). It comes from pickaxes, charms, mounts, armor sets, potions and the Core Tree, up to 100%.
  • Mining damage adds up from pickaxes, charms, mounts, sets and potions.
  • During a Meteor Shower some new blocks are meteorites (25% chance each, up to 8 per shower): break one for 2 gems.

Crafting a pickaxe with more pick power than your current one equips it at once. Your pickaxe also limits the crew: a crew member can never break a block your own pickaxe could not.
