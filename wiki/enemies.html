Enemies

Enemies get stronger the deeper you are. Each table shows an enemy's numbers at the first and the last depth of the layer (Normal difficulty, by day, first wave, no event).

Health and coins grow with depth as (1 + depth / 5)^1.15, attack as (1 + depth / 30). On top of that come night, events, Elite Waves and the difficulty; see Formulas. "Chance" is the drop chance per kill; drop chance bonuses multiply it.

Raiders

The Raider Warband event brings its own fighters. They have no fixed strength: they are a share of the average enemy of the layer you are in (Goblin raider: health x1, attack x1, coins x1.3, Bandit archer: health x0.7, attack x1.2, coins x1.3, Ogre brute: health x2.2, attack x1, coins x2.5). Spawn shares: Goblin raider 50%, Bandit archer 30%, Ogre brute 20%. Raiders drop no materials. Numbers at each layer's first depth:
