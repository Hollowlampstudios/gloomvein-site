Gems and rewards

Gems are the premium currency. They buy time, convenience and looks, never strength in a fight. Nothing for sale is random.

Free gems

  • Daily gift: 20 gems, +5 for every day in a row, up to +30.
  • Ads: 5 gems per ad, up to 10 ads a day.
  • The free chest: 5 gems each time (see below).
  • Bosses: gems for each boss's first win (see Bosses).
  • Achievements (see Achievements), meteorites and event wins (see Events).

What ads give (always optional)

  • Coin boost: all coins x2 for 10 minutes per ad, stacking up to 4 hours. It keeps running while the app is closed.
  • Free chest: every 45 minutes. It holds about 10 minutes of your world's income in coins (at least 25), 12 of the drop of each of your layer's two most common enemies, 2 of the best buff potion you know the recipe for, and 5 gems. Always these contents.
  • Welcome back x2: everything your crew dug and the bars your furnace made while you were away, paid a second time.
  • Boss retry: 15 more seconds on a boss timer.
