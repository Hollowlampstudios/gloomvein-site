How it works

Gloomvein has two halves: the battle at the top of the screen and the dig grid below it. They feed each other.

The dig

The grid is 7 blocks wide and 4 rows tall. Tap a block to hit it with your mining damage; when its health runs out it breaks, the column slides up and a new block appears at the bottom. Your depth is the number of blocks you have broken divided by 7.

Ore blocks need enough pick power (your best pickaxe). A block your pickaxe cannot break is dimmed and crossed out. Ore luck is the chance that an ore block gives two ores instead of one. See Mining.

Selling and the furnace

Ores and bars sell for coins in the Bag. Enemy drops sell for nothing: they are crafting materials. Sell All never sells what you are saving for: it keeps back enough of every material for the most expensive recipe you can see and have not built yet (and the next tier of gear, crew, spells or minions).

The furnace turns 2 ores into 1 bar on its own, one bar at a time. It smelts the most valuable bar it can, unless you tap the furnace strip and choose a bar to make first. See Furnace and materials.

The Workshop

Gear, potions, spells and minions cost coins and materials, so nothing can be bought with coins alone. A recipe appears once you have found every material it needs at least once (a bar counts as found as soon as you have its ore).

The battle

Your hero fights on their own. Enemies of the layer you are digging in walk in from the right, up to 3 at a time, one every 3.5 seconds. Tapping the battle does your class's special move (see Classes). Kills pay coins and sometimes drop materials.

Depth, Elite Waves and bosses

You cannot dig deeper than you have fought. The deepest depth you may reach is the number of Elite Waves you have beaten, plus one. Once you dig to that depth, every 10 kills bring an Elite Wave: 3 elite enemies with x1.8 health, x1.3 attack, x3 coins and x2 drop chance. Beat it and you may dig one level deeper. At a boss's depth the boss is the Elite Wave. Blocks keep breaking (and paying ore) while you wait; only the depth stops.

Each Elite Wave beaten also makes the next ordinary enemies 3% tougher, up to 9 steps.

Dying

Dying costs no coins and no items. You lie down for a few seconds (longer the deeper you are), your running potions end, the melee combo breaks and the count towards the next Elite Wave starts again.

The crew and time away

Crew members dig a column each, on their own. While the app is closed the crew keeps digging and the furnace keeps smelting what they bring, for up to 8 hours (more with a gem upgrade). There is no fighting while you are away.

Day and night

A day lasts 24 minutes. At night enemies are tougher but pay more. See The world.
