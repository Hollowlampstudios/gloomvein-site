Ice slime

Lives in Frostvale (depths 55 to 89), Sunscour (depths 90 to 134).

It attacks every 1.4 s. Like every enemy it grows stronger the deeper you are: the tables show it at the first and the last depth of each layer, by day and without events. Night, events and Elite Waves add to that (Formulas).
