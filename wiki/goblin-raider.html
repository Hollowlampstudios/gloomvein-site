Goblin raider

A raider: it comes with the Raider Warband event, in any layer.

Raiders have no fixed strength: this one has health x1, attack x1 and coins x1.3 of the average enemy of the layer you are in. Raiders drop no materials. Its numbers at each layer's first depth (Normal, by day):
