Old Marrow the Assayer

A crew member: put them on a column of the dig grid and they dig it for you, even while the app is closed.

  • Unlocks: Find lead
  • Dig 1.8 x level per second, pick power 2
  • Also: Ore luck +40%
  • Recruit: 30,000, 12 Lead bar, 10 Tin bar, 12 Gel
