Crew

Crew members dig for you. Put each one on a column of the dig grid; they dig it on their own, and keep digging while the app is closed.

  • Dig speed (damage per second) = their dig number x their level, doubled at levels 25, 50, 100 (so x8 from level 100).
  • A crew member breaks only blocks their own pickaxe allows and your pickaxe allows. Buy them better pickaxes below.
  • Crew ore luck works like yours: a chance of 2 ores from an ore block.
  • While Gorran is on a column, every other crew member digs 15% faster.
  • The Core Tree's Strong Crew and the gem shop's Crew boost x2 make the whole crew faster.

Each level costs the base coins x growth^(level - 1), rounded down. Every 5th level also costs materials: the amount shown x (level / 5).
