Events

Every so often something happens in the world. Random events start 10 to 20 minutes into play, then every 15 to 40 minutes. Seasons follow the calendar.

  • No event starts during a boss fight or just before one, and events never change bosses.
  • Effects of an event and a season multiply together.

Blood Moon

The moon turns red: tougher enemies, double coins and double drops.

  • Lasts 5 minutes; weight 3; only at night.
  • Effects: enemy health x1.4, enemy attack x1.25, coins x2, drop chance x2, enemies arrive x1.4.

Ooze Rain

Slimes fall from the sky: lots of slimes and extra gel.

  • Lasts 4 minutes; weight 3; only by day.
  • Effects: drop chance x1.6, enemies arrive x2.5.
  • Only the layer's slimes appear (if the layer has any).

Meteor Shower

Meteors land in the dig grid: dig them for gems.

  • Lasts 5 minutes; weight 2.
  • New dig blocks have a 25% chance to be a meteorite, up to 8; each pays 2 gems.

Raider Warband

A warband marches in: beat 25 raiders before time runs out for gems and coins.

  • Lasts 7 minutes; weight 2; after you beat Stormgaze.
  • Effects: enemies arrive x1.6, coins x1.5.
  • Raiders replace the layer's enemies. Beat 25 of them before time runs out for 6 gems and 1.5 chests' worth of coins (see Raiders).

Crystal Defence

Guard the crystal through 5 waves. Kills make energy: spend it on towers and repairs.

  • Lasts 10 minutes; weight 2; after you beat Sand Titan.
  • Effects: coins x1.5.
  • A crystal with 3 x your max health stands behind you and takes the enemies' hits. Survive 5 waves (sizes 8, 10, 12, 14, 16; each wave +12% tougher) to win 8 gems and 2 chests' worth of coins. If the crystal falls you get nothing.
  • You start with 5 energy and earn 1 per kill. Build up to 3 towers: Bolt (5 energy, every 1.2 s, attack x1, hits the front enemy); Frost (7 energy, every 1.5 s, attack x0.5, slows the front enemy); Arc (9 energy, every 2 s, attack x0.5, hits every enemy). Mend the crystal for 10 energy (+25%).

Halloween

A spooky week: 25% more coins and drops.

  • Every year from 10-25 to 11-01 (month-day).
  • Effects: coins x1.25, drop chance x1.25.

Winter Festival

Snow falls: 25% more coins and a double daily gem gift.

  • Every year from 12-18 to 01-02 (month-day).
  • Effects: coins x1.25, daily gem gift x2.
