Lives in Greenreach (depths 0 to 11), Rootdeep (depths 12 to 34), Glimmer Caverns (depths 35 to 54), Frostvale (depths 55 to 89), Sunscour (depths 90 to 134), Mirebloom (depths 135 to 184), The Crypt (depths 185 to 234).
