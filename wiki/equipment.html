Equipment

Gear is crafted in the Workshop with coins and materials. Stats add up across everything you wear.

  • Slots: pickaxe, weapon, head, body, legs, mount and up to 5 charms (depth 0, depth 0, depth 25, depth 60, depth 120), plus a sixth charm slot on Mythic.
  • A weapon, armor piece or mount of a higher tier than the one you wear goes on as soon as you craft it.
  • Crit chance is capped at 75%; a crit does x2 damage. Attacks can never come faster than every 0.25 s.
  • Defense: you take 10 / (10 + defense) of each hit, so 10 defense halves the damage and it never reaches zero.
  • Your class multiplies your attack (and for some classes health) by the highest weapon or armor tier you wear: see Classes.
