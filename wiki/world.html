"Enemy strength" is the layer's own multiplier on its enemies' health and attack (the deepest layers are a little gentler, because depth already makes everything tougher).

How deep you can go

The deepest depth you may reach is the number of Elite Waves you have beaten plus one; see How it works. Bosses stand at fixed depths; see Bosses.

Charm slots

Accessory (charm) slots open as you go deeper: depth 0: 2; depth 25: 3; depth 60: 4; depth 120: 5. A Mythic world has one more slot from the start.

Day and night

A full day is 24 minutes of play: day for 14.4 minutes, dusk 2.4, night 5.3, dawn 1.9. In full night enemies have +20% health and +15% attack, pay +50% coins, have +50% drop chance and arrive 30% faster; dusk and dawn blend in between. The clock in the game shows 06:00 at the start of each day.
