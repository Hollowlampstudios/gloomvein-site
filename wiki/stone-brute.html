Stone brute

Lives in Glimmer Caverns (depths 35 to 54), Frostvale (depths 55 to 89), Sunscour (depths 90 to 134).

It attacks every 1.6 s. Like every enemy it grows stronger the deeper you are: the tables show it at the first and the last depth of each layer, by day and without events. Night, events and Elite Waves add to that (Formulas).
