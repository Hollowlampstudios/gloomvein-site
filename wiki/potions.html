Potions

Potions are brewed in the Workshop and drunk from the potion bar in the battle.

  • A timed potion lasts 5 minutes. Drinking the same one again refreshes its timer; it never stacks. Different potions stack with each other.
  • Timed potions keep running while the app is closed.
  • Dying ends every running potion.
  • The gem shop's Potion duration upgrade makes every timed potion last 10 minutes, 20 minutes, 30 minutes (levels 1 to 3).
