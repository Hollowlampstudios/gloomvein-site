Bosses

A boss waits at a fixed depth. When you have dug down to it, 10 kills make it walk in: it is that depth's Elite Wave. Beat it before its timer runs out to go deeper; lose (time runs out or you fall) and the 10 kills start again.

  • The timer starts when the boss reaches you. After a while it enrages: its attack grows every second.
  • Its helpers are much weaker than the wild enemies at that depth, drop nothing, and your hero hits them first.
  • The reward is paid for the first victory only, once per world.
  • Ran out of time? Watch an ad (or pay gems) for 15 more seconds.
  • Events never change a boss, and no event starts during a boss fight or for 30 s after it.

Some bosses come in pairs and each world gets only one of each pair, picked when the world is made: Rotmaw or Veinheart; Dune Sovereign or Rime Queen.
