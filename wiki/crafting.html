Furnace and materials

The furnace makes bars from ore; bars and enemy drops make everything in the Workshop.

The furnace

It works on its own, one bar at a time, while you play and while you are away. It always makes the most valuable bar it has ore for, unless you tap the furnace strip and pick a bar: then it makes that one while the ore lasts, falls back to the most valuable one, and goes back to your pick when its ore comes in. The Core Tree's Hot Furnace makes it faster.
