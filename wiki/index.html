Gloomvein Wiki

The complete guide to Gloomvein, an idle mining RPG for Android. Dig down through 8 layers, fight what lives there, craft gear for one of 4 classes and beat the bosses that guard the way down.

Quick facts

  • 8 layers, from Greenreach at depth 0 to Cinder Depths from depth 235.
  • 30 enemies and 10 bosses; each world meets 8 of the bosses (some come in pairs, and a world gets one of each pair).
  • 4 classes, 100 pieces of equipment, 16 potions, 8 spells and 12 minions.
  • 3 difficulties: Normal, Heroic, Mythic.
  • Ads are never forced: every ad is one you choose to watch for a reward.

Numbers on this wiki are for the Normal difficulty, by day, outside events, unless a table says otherwise. The Formulas page shows how they change.
