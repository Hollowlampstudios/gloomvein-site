Brannoc the Stonecutter

A crew member: put them on a column of the dig grid and they dig it for you, even while the app is closed.

  • Unlocks: Reach depth 5
  • Dig 2.4 x level per second, pick power 1
  • Recruit: 2,400, 40 Stone, 10 Tin bar, 6 Tattered cloth
