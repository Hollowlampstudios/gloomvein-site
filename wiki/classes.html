Classes

You pick a class for each world. Gear made for another class does nothing for you, and only your own class's gear shows in the Workshop.

Melee

Hit hard and outlast everything

  • Your sword swings by itself
  • Tap for power hits - tap fast to build a combo (up to x1.6 damage)
  • The toughest class: the most health and a built-in defense

Rules

  • Tap damage is attack x 1.5, times (1 + combo). Each tap adds 0.1 to the combo, up to 0.6 (so up to x1.6); it fades 1.2 s after your last tap.
  • Heals 5% of the damage its sword deals (lifesteal).

Ranged

Shoot them before they arrive

  • Fires on its own from a distance, so enemies take hits while they walk up
  • Arrows are crafted from your bars: mining feeds the quiver
  • Out of arrows you still sling stones, weakly - never stuck

Rules

  • Shoots from range (62% of the arena). Each shot uses 1 arrow and adds the arrow's damage. Taps fire an extra shot at attack x 2.
  • With no arrows it slings stones for 40% damage. While the quiver is not empty it takes 30% less damage.
  • +1% of max health regenerated per second on top of the usual 1.5%.

Magic

Big spells, timed well

  • Staves fire mana bolts on their own; mana refills over time
  • Tap to cast your chosen spell - unlock more spells and pick between them
  • With no mana you only zap weakly - spend it wisely

Rules

  • Every automatic bolt costs 3 mana; with too little mana it zaps for 50% damage instead.
  • A tap casts your chosen spell; when it cannot be cast the tap does a 60% flick. Deals x1.65 damage to bosses.
  • +1.5% of max health regenerated per second on top of the usual 1.5%.

Summoner

Crack the whip, command the horde

  • Your whip attacks on its own, like a sword
  • Minions fight beside you; unlock more and bring up to your whip's limit
  • Tap to rally them: double damage for a few seconds

Rules

  • Minions each hit for attack x 0.6 x their power, every 1.1 s divided by ((1 + your attack speed bonus) x their speed).
  • A tap rallies them: x3 minion damage for 4 s, then 8 s cooldown (otherwise a 60% flick).
  • Minions soak up to 42% of the damage you take, in proportion to how many of your whip's minion slots are filled.
  • +2% of max health regenerated per second on top of the usual 1.5%.
